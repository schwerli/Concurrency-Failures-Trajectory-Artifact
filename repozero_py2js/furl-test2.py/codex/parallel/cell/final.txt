Implemented the pure ESM Node.js version in `/output`. It uses only local `.mjs` modules, manual `process.argv` parsing, and no external dependencies.

**Files**
- [argv.mjs](/output/lib/argv.mjs) handles `argparse`-style `--a` / `--b` parsing, help, required-argument errors, and unknown-argument errors.
- [host.mjs](/output/lib/host.mjs) normalizes and validates the replacement host value.
- [uri_reference.mjs](/output/lib/uri_reference.mjs) parses and serializes URL-like inputs to match the observed `furl(...).set(host=...).url` behavior.
- [errors.mjs](/output/lib/errors.mjs) formats Python-style runtime errors for invalid host cases.
- [furlish.mjs](/output/lib/furlish.mjs) exposes the host-setting wrapper used by the entry file.
- [test2.mjs](/output/test2.mjs) is the entry script.

**Verification**
- `node /output/test2.mjs` matched `/workspace/dataset/test2_executable` on a representative matrix of success cases exactly.
- CLI parse failures and invalid-host runtime failures also matched after the expected normalization of the executable basename (`test2.mjs` vs `test2_executable`) and process PID in the PyInstaller-style line.
