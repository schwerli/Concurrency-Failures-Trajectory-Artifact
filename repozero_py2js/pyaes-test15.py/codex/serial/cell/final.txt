**Files**
The ESM migration is written to `/output` and split by function:

- `lib/aes`: [field.mjs](/output/lib/aes/field.mjs), [tables.mjs](/output/lib/aes/tables.mjs), [key_schedule.mjs](/output/lib/aes/key_schedule.mjs), [core.mjs](/output/lib/aes/core.mjs), [ecb.mjs](/output/lib/aes/ecb.mjs)
- `lib/cli`: [arg_parser.mjs](/output/lib/cli/arg_parser.mjs)
- `lib/python`: [bytes_repr.mjs](/output/lib/python/bytes_repr.mjs), [traceback.mjs](/output/lib/python/traceback.mjs)
- Entry file: [test15.mjs](/output/test15.mjs)

All imports are local `.mjs` files. AES-ECB is reimplemented in pure JavaScript with no npm packages and no `node:crypto`.
