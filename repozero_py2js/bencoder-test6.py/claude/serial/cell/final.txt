## Key behaviors I reverse-engineered from the executable

- **Arbitrary-precision ints** — `--b 123456789012345678901234567890` round-trips, so integers are carried as `BigInt`, not `Number`.
- **Python `int()` parsing** — strips whitespace (including `\x1c`–`\x1f`/`\x85`, but *not* U+FEFF), accepts `+`/`-`, single underscores *between* digits, and any Unicode Nd block (`٤٢` → 42), while rejecting `0x10`, `1e3`, `1_`, `1__0`.
- **`bytes` repr quoting** — `b'x'` normally, `b"it's"` when the text has `'` but no `"`, and `b'a\'b"c'` when it has both; `\t\n\r` short escapes, `\xNN` below 0x20 and at/above 0x7f.
- **argparse fidelity** — `--a=v` form, unique-prefix abbreviation (`--he` → `--help`), last-occurrence wins, and one-pass option/value classification so `--a -5` succeeds while `--a -x` fails with *expected one argument*. Missing-required is reported before unrecognized; a bare `--` becomes an extra.

Two notes on that harness: it normalizes the program name, since `prog` comes from the script filename in both runtimes (`test6.mjs` vs `test6_executable`) and cannot match by construction.
