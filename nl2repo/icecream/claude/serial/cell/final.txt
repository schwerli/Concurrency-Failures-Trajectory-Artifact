## Two judgement calls worth flagging

1. **`executing`/`asttokens` weren't in the listed environment** — icecream cannot do source introspection without them. I `pip install`ed both and declared them in `pyproject.toml`; the tox run confirms a clean env gets them automatically.
2. **`icecream/icecream.py` sets `__path__`** so the spec's documented import line `import icecream.icecream.__version__ as version_module` works — that would otherwise be an `ImportError` (a module isn't a package). It's a deliberate shim, commented as such.

One spec inconsistency I resolved in favour of the spec's *code*: the prose example claims `ic(x, "hello", [1, 2])` → `ic| x: 42, 'hello': 'hello', [1, 2]: [1, 2]`, but the `_constructArgumentOutput` snippet in the same spec explicitly collapses literals to just their value. I implemented the collapsing behaviour (`ic| x: 42, 'hello', [1, 2]`), which is also what upstream icecream does.
