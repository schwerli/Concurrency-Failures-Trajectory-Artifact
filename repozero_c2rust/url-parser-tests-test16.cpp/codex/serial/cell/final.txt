Implemented the Rust port in [test16.rs](/output/test16.rs) with the library split across [parser.rs](/output/src/url_parser/parser.rs), [validate.rs](/output/src/url_parser/validate.rs), and [path.rs](/output/src/url_parser/path.rs), plus Cargo wiring in [Cargo.toml](/output/Cargo.toml). The behavior is intentionally matched to the C++ binary’s observed quirks: query/fragment stripping before parsing, scheme detection only on `://`, authority splitting on the first `/`, host/port splitting on the first `:`, and loose `stoi`-style port validation.

Verified with `cargo test`, `cargo build --release`, `rustc --edition=2021 test16.rs -o test16`, plain `rustc test16.rs -o test16_legacy`, a direct comparison against the provided C++ executable on the probe set, and a 2,000-case random differential fuzz pass. The compiled binaries are at `/output/test16` and `/output/target/release/test16`.
