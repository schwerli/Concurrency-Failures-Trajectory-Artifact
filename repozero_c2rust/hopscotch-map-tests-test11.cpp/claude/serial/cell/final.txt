## Behaviour recovered black-box

Probing the binary rather than the library source pinned three things that a naive port gets wrong:

- **`max_load_factor` defaults to `0.9f`, not `0.95`** — growth points are `trunc(bucket_count × 0.9f)`: 7, 14, 28, 57, 115, 230, 460, 921… (the non-constant ratio at 64+ buckets is the `float` truncation, confirmed up to 4096 buckets).
- **A default-constructed map has zero buckets**; the first insert grows to 2, and `load_factor()` returns `0` rather than NaN when there are none.
- **Output formatting needs exact-decimal round-half-to-even.** `33/128 = 0.2578125` is an exact tie at 6 significant digits and must print `0.257812`, while `0.2734375` must print `0.273438`. Rust's `{}`/`{:.6}` can't do this, so `cxx::decimal` expands the float exactly via `m·2⁻ᵏ = (m·5ᵏ)/10ᵏ` on a decimal bignum.
