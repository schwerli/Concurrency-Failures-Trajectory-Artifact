## Behavior recovered black-box from the binary

The C++ library is a **pruned** Rails inflector, and several of its quirks only show up under probing:

- **`pluralize` has no rule table at all** — it appends `s` unless the word is empty or one of exactly 10 uncountables (`equipment information rice money species series fish sheep jeans police`), matched case-sensitively against the whole string. Hence `boxs`, `citys`, `classs`, and `Sheep` → `Sheeps`.
- **`singularize` keeps only 11 rules.** Verified *absent*: `(shoe)s`, `(tive)s`, `(hive)s`, `(m)ovies`, `(s)eries`, `(bus)(es)?`, `(alias|status)`, `(octop|vir)`, `(cris|test)`, `(ss)$`, `^(ox)en` — which is why `shoes`→`sho`, `hives`→`hife`, `movies`→`movy`, `class`→`clas`.
- **A real bug reproduced:** `singularize("analyses")` → **`analyasis`**, not `analysis`. The `((a)naly|(b)a|…)(sis|ses)$` rule expands to `$1$2sis` instead of `$1sis`; group 2 only participates in the `analy` branch, so that one stem picks up a stray `a`. Every sibling (`bases`→`basis`, `theses`→`thesis`) is unaffected. Documented at `src/inflection/rules.rs:139`.
- **`titleize` splits on the space character only** — not tabs, not underscores (`a_b`→`A_b`, `a\tb`→`A\tb`) — and lower-cases word tails (`FISH`→`Fish`).
- **`parameterize` drops non-alphanumerics** rather than collapsing them: spaces become dashes, everything else vanishes, and runs are neither collapsed nor trimmed (`The  Test`→`the--test`, `a_b`→`ab`).
- **`classify` does not underscore first** — proven by `A B_table`→`A BTable` (underscoring first would give `A _bTable`).
- **All character ops are byte-wise ASCII**, so UTF-8 passes through untouched (`ÉLAN` → `underscore` → `É_l_a_n`). Internals work on `&[u8]`/`Vec<u8>` to preserve this exactly.

Compared stdout+stderr+exit code against `test13_executable` in every case. The cargo and `rustc` binaries produce identical output.

One deliberate judgment call: three Rails rules (`(m|l)ice$`, `([ti])a$`, `^(ox)en`) are **unobservable** through this harness, since `singularize` is only ever called with `word + "s"` and those patterns require a non-`s` ending. Given how many neighboring rules were verifiably pruned, I omitted them to match the inferred shape of the library rather than guessing them in. No input this program can construct reaches them either way.
