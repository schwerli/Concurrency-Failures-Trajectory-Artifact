Implemented the Rust 2021 port in `/output` as a complete Cargo project with the entry binary at [test20.rs](/output/test20.rs) and modular inflector logic in [lib.rs](/output/lib.rs), [inflection/mod.rs](/output/inflection/mod.rs), [inflection/case.rs](/output/inflection/case.rs), and [inflection/transform.rs](/output/inflection/transform.rs). The implementation is byte-oriented and preserves the observed quirks of the C++ binary, including the buggy `demodulize` behavior and the abort on an empty string argument.

Builds now succeed with both `cargo build --release` and `rustc --edition=2021 test20.rs -o test20`. The standalone executable is at `/output/test20`, and the Cargo release binary is at `/output/target/release/test20`. I verified stdout and exit behavior against `/workspace/dataset/test20_executable` on representative inputs including `cat`, `dogs`, `foo_bar`, `FooBar`, spaced and punctuated inputs, `ümlaut`, and `""`.
