Implemented a standalone reimplementation in [src/zoxide.py](/workspace/src/zoxide.py) with [compile.sh](/workspace/compile.sh) installing it to `./executable` and creating a `./zoxide` symlink. It covers the observed `add`, `query`, `remove`, `import`, `edit` (including the hidden `reload/delete/increment/decrement` subcommands), frecency scoring, `_ZO_*` env handling, default data-dir layout, and shell init generation for the documented shells.

I verified the replacement against the reference binary before overwriting it on a representative matrix: help/version text, add/query ordering and matching rules, no-match/error cases, import formats for `z` and `autojump`, unavailable-directory handling, symlink resolution, maxage aging behavior, and the interactive `fzf` failure paths. After running `./compile.sh`, I also sanity-checked the built `./executable` directly. The main remaining risk is that `init` output was matched structurally rather than exhaustively byte-for-byte for every shell/option combination.
