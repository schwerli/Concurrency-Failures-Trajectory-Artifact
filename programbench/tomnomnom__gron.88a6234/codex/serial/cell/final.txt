Implemented a clean-room Go reimplementation in [main.go](/workspace/main.go), with [compile.sh](/workspace/compile.sh) building a standalone replacement at [executable](/workspace/executable). I preserved the supplied binary as [original-executable](/workspace/original-executable) for comparison only; the new executable does not depend on it.

I verified the replacement against the original across the observed surface: normal flattening, `--json`, `--stream`, `--ungron`, `--values`, help/version, exit codes, parse errors, number lexeme preservation, string escaping, HTTP fetch/proxy behavior, and merge edge cases. The probe suite matched end-to-end. The only non-byte-stable area is `--no-sort`, because the original itself relies on unsorted Go map iteration.
