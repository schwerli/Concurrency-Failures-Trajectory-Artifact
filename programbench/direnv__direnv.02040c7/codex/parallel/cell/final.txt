Reimplemented the tool from scratch as a Go codebase with an embedded bash stdlib. The main CLI and command surface are in [main.go](/workspace/main.go), state/config handling in [config.go](/workspace/config.go), diff/watch encoding and shell renderers in [env.go](/workspace/env.go), `.envrc`/`.env` evaluation in [eval.go](/workspace/eval.go), network fetch support in [fetchurl.go](/workspace/fetchurl.go), and the stdlib exposed by `direnv stdlib` in [stdlib.sh](/workspace/stdlib.sh). [compile.sh](/workspace/compile.sh) now rebuilds `./executable` from source.

I rebuilt `./executable` and smoke-tested the behaviors I reverse-engineered from the original binary: help/version, hooks, blocked/allowed/denied exports, repeat-noop behavior, unloads, `exec`, `status`, `dump`/`apply_dump`, `fetchurl`, and `require_allowed` approval flows.
