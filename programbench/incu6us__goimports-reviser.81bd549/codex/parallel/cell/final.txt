Implemented a clean-room Go reimplementation of the probed CLI in [main.go](/workspace/main.go), [config.go](/workspace/config.go), [imports.go](/workspace/imports.go), [packageinfo.go](/workspace/packageinfo.go), and [processor.go](/workspace/processor.go). [compile.sh](/workspace/compile.sh) now rebuilds `./executable` with `GO111MODULE=off` so the workspace itself does not gain a root-module behavior the original binary never had.

I verified the rebuilt `./executable` against the behavior matrix I mapped from the original: help/version output, no-input and no-`go.mod` failures, `stdout`/`file`/`write` modes, `-list-diff`, `-set-exit-status`, recursive walking and excludes, generated-file skipping, import grouping/order, `-rm-unused`, `-set-alias`, and the observed formatting quirks. Probe fixtures are still in `/workspace/tmp_*` if you want to inspect the comparison cases.
