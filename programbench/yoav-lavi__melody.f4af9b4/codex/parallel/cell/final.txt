Reimplementation is in [melody_cli.py](/workspace/melody_cli.py), with shell completion payloads in [data/completions](/workspace/data/completions) and install/build logic in [compile.sh](/workspace/compile.sh). `compile.sh` replaces `./executable` with the new standalone script and preserves the observed binary as `./executable.original` for local comparison.

It matches the observed CLI, REPL, compiler, and test behavior I could probe, including the undocumented range/class forms (`a to z`, `not abc`), lazy/open-ended quantifiers (`lazy`, `over`), groups/assertions, named captures, and completion output. I verified it by differential testing against `./executable.original` on fixed edge cases plus randomized valid Melody programs; the final smoke check passed.
