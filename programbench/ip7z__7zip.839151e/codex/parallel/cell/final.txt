Implemented a fresh reimplementation that `compile.sh` installs as `./executable`. The active entrypoint is [reimpl.py](/workspace/reimpl.py), the CLI behavior lives in [app.py](/workspace/src/sevenza/app.py), and the 7z read/write layer is in [libarchive7z.py](/workspace/src/sevenza/libarchive7z.py). It covers the observed help/error surface, `a/u/d/rn/l/t/x/e/h/i/b`, stdin/stdout modes, and the archive types exercised during probing: `7z`, `zip`, `tar`, `gzip`, `bzip2`, and `xz`.

Verified locally through the rebuilt `./executable` for `--help`, bad switch/command exit codes, missing and invalid archive handling, 7z create/list/test/extract flows, `-si` and `-so`, and hashing. I also cleaned the workspace back down to the actual codebase and disabled Python bytecode cache writes so repeated runs do not mutate future archive scans. The main known gap is that some multi-file 7z internal listing metadata, especially exact header/packed-size accounting, is still approximate rather than byte-for-byte identical.
