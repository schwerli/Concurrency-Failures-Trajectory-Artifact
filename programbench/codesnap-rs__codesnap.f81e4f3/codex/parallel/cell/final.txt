Implemented a clean-room reimplementation in [codesnap.py](/workspace/codesnap.py) with [compile.sh](/workspace/compile.sh) installing it as `./executable`. It recreates the observed CLI surface, default config generation, PNG/SVG output, gradient/window snapshot rendering, execution mode, range handling, line numbers, breadcrumbs, title, add/delete/highlight bands, ASCII warning behavior, and the headless clipboard failure path.

Verified in this environment with `./executable --version`, `-c`, `-f`, `--silent`, `--type ascii`, `-e ... --skip`, and clipboard/error cases, plus a final smoke test generating `/workspace/final_smoke.png`. Residual risk is in the less common flags I didn’t re-check exhaustively after switching the final build to the dependency-free Python implementation.
