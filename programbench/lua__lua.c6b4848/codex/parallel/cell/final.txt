Implemented a clean-room replacement in [cleanlua.py](/workspace/cleanlua.py), with [executable](/workspace/executable) as the final entrypoint and [compile.sh](/workspace/compile.sh) as the build step. The replacement covers the observed CLI contract (`-e`, `-l`, `-i`, `-v`, `-E`, `-W`, `--`, `-`), `LUA_INIT` handling, `arg` layout, stdin/REPL behavior, and a substantial Lua runtime surface including tables/metatables, coroutines, `load`/`loadfile`, `require`, warnings, and the base/string/table/math/io/os/debug/utf8 libraries used in probing.

I verified representative behaviors against the provided binary before removing the oracle copy: version/help/error output, implicit vs explicit stdin exit codes, `-i` with piped input, env-init ordering, `string.dump`/`load(..., 'b')`, coroutine wrapping/yield semantics, and custom-env `load`. Remaining risk is breadth, not the core contract: this is a large clean-room reimplementation, but it is not exhaustively validated against every possible Lua 5.5 program.
