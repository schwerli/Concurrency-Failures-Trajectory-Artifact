Implemented a clean-room replacement in [src/seqtk_reimpl.py](/workspace/src/seqtk_reimpl.py) with a matching build entrypoint in [compile.sh](/workspace/compile.sh). I also kept the black-box fixtures I used in [fixtures](/workspace/fixtures).

Verification was done side-by-side against the provided binary by running the Python entrypoint directly, so I did not execute `compile.sh` here because it would overwrite the observation target. On the fixture suite I probed, the replacement matched the original for the top-level CLI, `seq`, `size`, `comp`, `subseq`, default-seed `sample`, `split`, `mergepe`, `dropse`, `trimfq`, `rename`, `hpc`, `mutfa`, `famask`, `mergefa`, `randbase` on the mixed-ambiguity case, `listhet`, `gap`, `cutN`, `hety`, `gc`, and the observed `telo` behavior.

Residual risk remains in less-constrained paths I didn’t fully pin down from the binary, especially non-default `sample` seeds, some `randbase` sequences, and `fqchk`’s exact `errQ` calculation.
