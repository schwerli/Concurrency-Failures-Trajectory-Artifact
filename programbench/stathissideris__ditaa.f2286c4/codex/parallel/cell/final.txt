Implemented a clean-room replacement in [src/ditaa_reimpl.py](/workspace/src/ditaa_reimpl.py) with a simple build step in [compile.sh](/workspace/compile.sh). The new [executable](/workspace/executable) reproduces the observed CLI contract, writes PNG by rendering through `libgd`, and emits SVG directly from the parsed diagram model.

I verified the main observable paths: `-h`, no-arg usage, the odd `--help` failure, unknown-option handling, PNG/SVG generation, default/explicit output naming, and HTML mode output generation. Residual risk remains on rendering fidelity for more complex nested or non-rectangular diagrams, where the original binary’s shape inference is more nuanced than this reimplementation.
